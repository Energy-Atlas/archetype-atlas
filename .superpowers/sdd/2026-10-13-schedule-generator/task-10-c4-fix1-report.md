Existing bundle validation did not close those nested identities.

The merge now copies the complete incoming bundle and calls the existing strict `remap_binding_references` with its exact full local-to-global map before preserving generation receipts or appending bindings/owners. Keys, schedule IDs, order, source/target/RNG and public generic allocation semantics are retained. Validation walks explicit generated reference fields in bindings, constituents and generation, rejecting Boolean/dangling indices and conflicting recorded owner/schedule/demand identity. Receipts with method `actual_generated_bound_trajectory_sum` require complete binding/owner/schedule/demand component identity; arbitrary original fields without generated indices are unaffected. Valid public separately generated source-parent composition remains supported.

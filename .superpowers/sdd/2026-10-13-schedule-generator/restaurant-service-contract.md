Inspection: 2026-10-14. This is a bounded, read-only contract study for `FullServiceRestaurant` and `QuickServiceRestaurant`; it is not a source-native comparison, implementation approval, or release decision. No source download, Ruby/OpenStudio run, or generator test was made. ## Original main and booster draws

The main branches are **space-type fixture draws**. `Standards.ServiceWaterHeating.rb` lines 122-171 iterates the model's source `space_type_map` and each named space. Lines 236-305 use the helper default `is_flow_per_area: true`, multiply `service_water_heating_peak_flow_per_area` by **each actual space's floor area and multiplier once**, convert gal/h → gal/min → m³/s, and create the fixture without passing a `space:` argument. The catalogue's `assignment=consumer_host_when_applicable` points to the source Kitchen space type; its `zone_heat_assignment=unresolved` correctly does not prove a thermal-zone heat destination. The `source_space_names=['Kitchen']`, `physical_fixture_locations=[]` distinction in the completion path is material. A heater's reported Kitchen location is likewise not the fixture's physical attachment. For repeated actual Kitchen spaces, identify each physical fixture separately; one catalogue service ID is a source path identity and must not collapse distinct real fixtures.

## Schedule and ownership integration

Water has three **different** frozen source schedules, not a restaurant-wide alias. Full main and booster share `RestaurantSitDown BLDG_SWH_SCH`, `schedule-3b2dac30655f17d22eeb`, source JSON `ashrae_90_1.schedules.json#/schedules/13476..13478`. Quick code templates use `RestaurantFastFood BLDG_SWH_SCH`, `schedule-cd713af94aeb6baa09ce`, `/13344..13346`; Quick DOE templates use `QuickServiceRestaurant Bldg Swh`, `schedule-88fd74bb51fc6312d361`, `/13281..13283`. That source schedule file is SHA-256 `51240ccea12e2c626017c937c05ace3cd3e5085b60279d78522d29ac58d7719b`. Each has three 24-value hourly rules and source 2014 date range, but distinct day selectors and values. Preserve source order, selectors and design-day rules. The catalogue `schedules.json` `provenance_id` and completion schedule evidence carry the original rows; its source fractions remain the annual reference shape. No water-service event process or ComStock water-use sampler is in this pinned method.

The source **People** HOO carrier is different from the water schedule. Code Full Dining/Kitchen share `RestaurantSitDown BLDG_OCC_SCH` (`schedule-7721bc575c41621510fb`); DOE Full share `FullServiceRestaurant Bldg Occ` (`schedule-616fb33ba79eef5a29d8`). Code Quick share `RestaurantFastFood BLDG_OCC_SCH` (`schedule-4cfbf4794a826f3b1e86`); DOE Quick share `QuickServiceRestaurant Bldg Occ` (`schedule-68b89a580153cba49294`). Dining and Kitchen have positive design densities; Attic source occupancy is unknown with no source schedule. For 2013, Dining is `0.7534737291696806 person/m²`, Kitchen `0.05381955208354861 person/m²`; the exact original People object, actual source/caller area, and multiplier must still be bound. The Standards 0.8.6 `parametric.rb` selects the nonresidential or residential group using source space-type names and summed design people (lines 23-165), and `space.rb` aggregates each eligible People Ruleset with `people.getNumberOfPeople(space.floorArea)` and six-decimal weighted-hour rounding (lines 423-518). A common schedule name does not by itself prove an area-free equivalent HOO carrier. Bind the actual Dining and Kitchen People/areas, or validate a proposed normalized carrier against native 0.8.6 for that exact source case and threshold. Bind source-room classification, group, calendar, holidays, design people, multiplier, and source order; Attic needs an explicit absent/unknown receipt, not zero people by label. The 0.8.6 gem SHA-256 is `9a3477cdb6aac7501430a214cc1be5fc93c28b8ddd444e39579d442931e3a19f` and its `parametric.rb` member SHA-256 is `997167daf4dad43fd5e375b9b2471ac9486c814854882bcdaa4de864c207501c`.

The original fixture helper creates water equipment **without an attached space**. `parametric.rb` lines 309-325, if the flow fraction is an eligible Ruleset and has no prior parametric entry, uses `spaces_hours_of_operation(model.getSpaces)` for that water schedule. Full main/booster can point to the same schedule object, and the method explicitly skips a duplicate `parametric_inputs` key. This supports one source schedule transform with two separate original flow budgets if a native comparison confirms eligibility and resulting rules. It does **not** support assigning the water draw to Kitchen HOO just because Kitchen is the space-type beneficiary. The v33 ComStock `full_service_restaurant` / `quick_service_restaurant` selectors sample operating hours and lighting/plug BPR; their electric, lighting and occupancy measures do not themselves redefine water volume. Therefore classify the fixture reference as source-fixed until its actual Standards parametric branch and HOO carrier are reproduced and compared; then record `source_parametric` or an unchanged outcome per exact schedule/rule. Never apply a plug/lighting BPR or occupancy multiplier to the water draw by analogy. The conditional restaurant plug/lighting CDF strict-tail errors in the prior audit remain source errors; do not normalize, retry or fill tails.

Source/reviewed IDs, templates and service objects are alternatives at the catalogue selection boundary. The main Kitchen `loads[]` and `services[]` are two views of one source demand. Full main and booster are simultaneous independent fixture volumes where enabled. Within an actual model, `instantiate_once` means one binding per physical fixture identity, with repeat references shown as links. It does not merge two distinct Kitchen spaces that happen to use the same service definition. A reviewed Dining-only instantiation has no evidence for allocating the omitted Kitchen water service to Dining; if the intended result is a whole-building service, supply a separate service owner and physical extent, retaining an `already_allocated` receipt if an upstream total has been distributed. No restaurant composition weights exist to invent a share. A source-space fixture is a whole fixture at its own physical scope; an apportioned part of a whole-building source service is a component and requires total/component/already-allocated proof before combination.

## Frozen v0.4 runtime gap and concrete adapter proposal

The inspected `data/generator-releases/v0.4.0/generator.zip` has SHA-256 `fb60c9231143af220739bb1947d9ff12262548ab5d449ffd5b90376324090531`. Its `atlas_schedules/generator.py` lines 63-79 rejects programme IDs outside `m1-scope` (restaurants are absent from both scope and `catalogue-index`); `programs.py` lines 19-70 requires a compiled exact recipe. `budgets.py` lines 8-14, 35-70 and 73-107 already accepts `m3/s/m2` and `m3/s`, computes source-schedule volume with the 3,600 s/hour factor, and binds the emitted fraction to the same annual target. `companions.py` lines 70-76 can emit source Ruleset companions. `mixtures.py` lines 187-216 can deduplicate matching `mode=whole` physical services with explicit extent/denominator, and rejects other modes with `requires_binding`; it compares source ID, demand, target, extent and trajectory, then emits once. Its catalogue composition path is still rejected (lines 128-134), which is immaterial to these families because no restaurant compositions exist. It has **no restaurant source recipe, family-qualified water transform receipt, source HOO binding, or supported `component`/`already_allocated` service allocation**. `basis_bindings` area aliases and multiplicity only express caller physical scale; they cannot supply source geometry or an omitted Kitchen owner by themselves. `generate()` currently samples one operating group per caller group and refuses conflicting source/configuration/carrier context (lines 87-103), so Full/Quick may share machinery but require separate group contexts and sampled receipts.

An implementation-ready sequence, with each template/view independently qualified, is:

1. Start with the 90.1-2013 **source** three-record set in each family (Dining, Kitchen, Attic), not an assumed 46-record batch. Resolve every known load/control slot from that exact programme's frozen original `schedule_id`, provenance, value, basis and status. Dining/Kitchen lighting and plug are programme-specific; Kitchen gas equipment is a distinct fuel load. Unknown additional lighting, Dining gas, Attic occupancy/equipment/controls and any no-schedule slot remain explicit unknown/absent cases until source evidence resolves them. Preserve source-fixed constant/design days and each load's original annual target.
2. Build the source HOO carrier from the two actual positive-People objects, including source room/space-type names, actual `floor_area_m2`, design people and multipliers, or provide a source-native compared normalized carrier. Sample the correct v33 selector (`full_service_restaurant` or `quick_service_restaurant`) with original conditional table row/draw receipts and an explicit non-leap local-standard-time calendar. Keep population-realization equivalence false. Compare occupancy, thermostat, electric and lighting rules separately with native methods; never borrow the other family's profile or a Warehouse/Office carrier.
3. Bind Kitchen main `service_id` to the exact `path_id`, `beneficiary_program_ids`, `ownership`, `assignment`, source Kitchen fixture identity and the **actual physical Kitchen extent**. Emit one hot-water volume demand with its source fraction/annual target and unresolved zone heat assignment; do not emit both the `loads[]` and `services[]` aliases. Independently bind the Full booster in the three enabled templates as an absolute, unallocated building service with its own physical ID and volume target, retaining the shared water-schedule transform if source-proved. Do not assign booster to Kitchen merely from heater location. A caller whole-service mix needs a real denominator/extent; any split needs component/total allocation evidence unavailable in v0.4.
4. Qualify the reviewed view as its own Dining/Attic record set and prove how the omitted Kitchen fixture is represented if a **whole-building** result is requested. Then repeat for the other four templates, retaining code versus DOE service schedules and booster differences. Do not auto-include a source Kitchen programme in reviewed view. Each successful receipt should contain exact programme ID/view/template, source file/checksum/locator, source schedule rules, HOO group/member People and space context, v33 selector/rows/draws, per-slot method/status, physical host, service ID/path/physical owner, magnitude original unit/value/transformation, annual-volume budget and emitted effective magnitude. `requires_binding` is the correct outcome for missing extent/host/allocation; fixed known schedules get `source_fixed`; null catalogue fields remain unknown rather than zero.

The two restaurant families can use **one proven service adapter interface** for source-space main fixtures and optional absolute booster, plus the existing budget and whole-service machinery. Qualification remains separate for each family, template and view because the occupancy and water rules, DOE/code distinctions, booster presence, and source conditional rows differ. This report does not approve a plan-wide scope adjustment or certify any of the 46 records for release.

American Community Survey (ACS) Public Use Microdata Sample (PUMS), U.S.
Census Bureau (https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/microdata.html).
A work of the U.S. federal government, in the public domain in the United
States. Used here, as pinned in sources/occupancy-mix-lock.json, to derive
state household-population compositions (scripts/derive_occupancy_mix.py).
Please cite the U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey PUMS, when using
derived results.

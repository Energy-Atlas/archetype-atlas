American Time Use Survey (ATUS) microdata, U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
(https://www.bls.gov/tus/). A work of the U.S. federal government, in the
public domain in the United States. Used here, as pinned in
sources/occupancy-mix-lock.json, to derive occupant-type probabilities per
demographic cell (scripts/derive_occupancy_mix.py). Please cite the Bureau of
Labor Statistics, American Time Use Survey, when using derived results.

DOE/PNNL Prototype Building Models (commercial ASHRAE 90.1 and IECC, residential IECC,
manufactured housing)

Source: U.S. Department of Energy Building Energy Codes Program,
        https://www.energycodes.gov/prototype-building-models
Models: developed by Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL) for DOE; the IDF headers cite
        "DOE and PNNL".

The archives carry no separate licence statement. energycodes.gov is a DOE website whose footer
links the energy.gov Web Policies (https://www.energy.gov/web-policies), which treat government
information on DOE websites as public domain, excluding third-party material, and ask that DOE
be acknowledged. The atlas therefore records these files as U.S. government work in the public
domain with attribution requested, and credits the U.S. Department of Energy and PNNL wherever
values derived from them are used.

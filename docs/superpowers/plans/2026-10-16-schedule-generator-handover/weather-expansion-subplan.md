## Normalized coverage and proof contract

For a future scope, define an explicit ordered `case` tuple `(programme_id, method_id, source_family, evidence_view, template, source_configuration_id, location_context_id or null, weather_variant_id or null, calendar_id, caller_operand_schema_id)`. The method's dependency declaration controls whether location and weather are required. The selected M1 interior commercial method may use `weather: not_used` only with its exact cited audit. New HVAC, refrigeration, water, controls, 2019, or stock methods require their own dependency inspection and source proof. A missing climate dependency is `unknown`, not wildcard. Group identical programme rules only after comparing original selectors, schedule objects, values, source order, rule dates/day selectors/design days, source method and physical targets; keep an applicability relation keyed by actual source IDs/contexts. Do not duplicate identical recipes into 16 or 37 rows for display.

Maintain separate registers: (1) source-defined *needed contexts*, (2) locked available weather/location inputs, (3) method-qualified programme/context channels, and (4) public declared release cases. A coverage status is per channel and case: `generated`, `source_fixed`, `absent`, `requires_binding`, or `unsupported`, with separate `source_state` (`known`, `unknown`, `absent`, `shared`, `excluded`), exclusion reason and evidence. An absent source load needs an exact zero/skip/disabled receipt; null stays unknown. A source-fixed schedule requires exact rule/default and provenance proof; a frozen realization alone is insufficient. A shared service retains one physical identity, host, fuel, basis and beneficiary/assignment links. A composition becomes ready only if all ordered member occurrences and shared services are qualified for one compatible ResourceSet and the caller's exact area/people/multiplicity bindings exist. Do not count an unsupported or excluded channel as complete.

For each record, retain upstream project/revision, immutable archive/member SHA, source path and field pointer/line, original value/unit, transformation to SI, extraction date, interpretation, method branch, source/view/template, climate/location/weather variant, calendar, and source order. Local-standard-time non-leap 2007 remains the existing case calendar; a different year requires an explicit versioned scope and source validation. `sources/weather-lock.json` identifies a TMY3 **station-proxy** archive, not a DOE reference EPW lock. A later DOE source lock must identify the official archive/version, license notice, exact archive/member/EPW hashes and sizes, report representative city and actual weather-file location separately, and use immutable cache retrieval. No filename may be guessed from a city label.

For weather-dependent residential methods, the pinned `atlas_schedules/residential/shapes.py` uses state/month shifts, latitude/longitude/UTC offset for interior/garage lighting, and 8,760 EPW dry-bulb hours to select ceiling-fan months; the HMM core occupants use source RNG and do not require EPW. `atlas_schedules/residential/selected.py` uses only the four-field L tuple for the selected occupants/interior-light method, with no weather input, and rejects positive N outside L37. These are different methods. Heating/cooling season placement, weather-mains water temperature and appliance-location temperature must be investigated in their actual source branches; outdoor EPW alone cannot stand in for a refrigerator location temperature. Preserve EV and HVAC unavailability exclusions.

For each load, retain original annual/reference target, original magnitude, fuel/host/basis and schedule; compute effective magnitude from the **final emitted** 8,760 fractions so annual Wh, Wh/m² or m³ meets the original per-channel target. Do not fit before later weather shape changes, multiply fractions beyond valid bounds, assign a new annual budget to a composed parent, or turn non-energy occupancy/activity/temperature controls into energy demands. Preserve actual seed/RNG draw order and native unit order; commercial operating groups share their selected sample and must retain source selectors. Reuse accepted exact matching proofs only if the complete source/method/context/cut signature matches; derive missing observations as `required_case_keys − accepted_case_keys`, with each required channel/seed/branch included. A quota count is not a substitute for this set difference.

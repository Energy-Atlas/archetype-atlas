## Constraints and review focus

- Source geometry is derivation evidence, not consumer geometry or a program dependency.
- SI canonical ACH is 1/s; inspection ACH is 1/h. Null never means zero.
- Keep full climate labels, source aliases and distinct reference forms; no averaging or merging.
- Commercial denominator is the volume of the whole source enclosure, with source multipliers, including interior spaces/plenums. This is not labelled conditioned volume.
- Preserve NIST no-air-barrier/default leakage, coefficient fallback and climate-0 fallback; exclude operating additions.
- Preserve source EnergyPlus centroid, terrain and weather-station height rules. Verify defaults/overrides rather than using mid-height or raw weather wind.
- Residential exterior exposure is applied once, already included in the recovered sensitivity.
- Whole-SmallHotel benchmark mapping must retain the absent GuestRoom scope flag.
- No full program/catalogue/site build or CI; frozen releases stay immutable.

# ADR 0025: Apartment prototype temporal hybrid

> Amended by ADR 0035 (2026-10-17): the semantic decisions stand; bit-level exactness, authority locks and certification ceremony are superseded.

Status: accepted architecture boundary for progressive Task 10. A1 selected timing and A2 source recipes are build-only components; production qualification remains closed.

The controller approved the source proposal documented in `.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-13-schedule-generator/apartment-method-audit.md` on 2026-10-14. The exact 84 HighriseApartment/MidriseApartment records remain separate by room, template and source/reviewed view. There are 40 dwelling records, 28 corridors and 16 support offices. No source-defined apartment composition or ComStock v33 apartment selector exists in the pinned evidence.

For a caller-bound dwelling unit, the pinned ResStock individual-unit HMM may supply occupancy timing with a separately explicit integer resident count. Preserve the prototype design density and activity separately from chain residents; do not infer residents from area or copy fixture occupants. Solar/active-at-home lighting may supply unit lighting timing, preserving each original main/additional annual budget separately. The selected A1 method consumes a complete locked state/latitude/longitude/physical-UTC-offset tuple, positive integer N, the 2007 local-standard calendar and the ordered source unit seed. It consumes no weather, bedroom or installed-appliance flag. Both existing whole residential wrappers remain weather dependent. The 37 timing tuples are controlled regression contexts, never prototype location defaults.

The A2 candidate compiler retains aggregate electric equipment and fixed DHW on their exact original rules. Event allocation is a later, separately evidenced adaptation; undecomposed appliances remain unknown. Corridor and apartment support-office schedules use their own exact fixed source methods. Neither corridor HMM nor generic medium_office sampling is authorized. Source-null gas/additional lighting, reviewed zero and absent loads remain distinct.

Water-service ownership remains instantiate-once, with the exact physical service path, original whole-service extent and unresolved zone-heat assignment retained. Source/reviewed views of a service never create duplicate water draws. Whole-building composition requires caller member areas, multiplicities and explicit physical-service identities because the source catalogue provides none here.

The A2 candidate scope is schema-versioned JSON with the ordered exact 84 programme IDs and original locators, the 37 complete A1 regression tuples, whole-file SHA-256 locks and explicit known/unknown/zero/absent policy. The Python compiler reopens those original definition and raw source files, retains whole programme/rule/row objects and per-field provenance, and recompiles all 40 water requirements independently. Generated controls and associations are canonical-hashed in memory from those verified originals; their `derived/apartment-a2/` paths identify candidate build objects, not separately shipped proofs. The recipes retain original fixed references and targets, with 2007 local-standard rule evaluation. Positive dwelling N is an unbounded integer domain subject to later private validation, while the uint64 master seed belongs to the top-level `generate(params, seed)` contract. A4 must validate the original recipe and complete location tuple before RNG; the generic finite-choice `variable_inputs` resolver cannot express this domain. A2 records no finite substitute allowlist and opens no runtime path.

The cost is an explicit adaptation contract and caller allocation/binding validation, separate cohort/design-count receipts, fixed method classification, unit-level native RNG/branch captures and conservation tests. A1's archived selected evidence comprises 55 ordinary HMM targets and three controlled component targets with substituted state matrices after genuine activity draws. The four selected-method cases passed their named hourly fraction comparison. These tests establish the selected subgraph within the recorded arguments, not per-prototype programme equivalence. Missing operands are `requires_binding`; no native certification or complete apartment support is claimed by this ADR alone. Original Program JSON v3 and historical frozen releases stay immutable.

# ADR 0021: Source-ready independent above-ground construction JSON

Date: 2026-10-14. Status: implemented and frozen on `feature/json-completion`;
protected-main publication remains pending.

## Decision

Implement construction JSON 1.0.0 independently of program and systems. Central
material and provenance resources support ordered opaque, simple-glazing and
detailed-glazing assemblies. Commercial packages retain the complete source
selector census, including explicit absent and deferred slots. Residential
enclosures retain all above-ground source recipes and scaling/adjacency rules.
ADR 0020's accepted fixed natural ACH belongs to package/enclosure references.
Foundation-dependent assemblies and all systems remain deferred.

Determine eligibility from native source closure, not the previous extracted
subset. Preserve all 3,206 eligible frozen IDs and retain every newly recovered
dependency/variant: 5,092 ready identities comprise 4,868 assemblies, 183 packages
and 41 residential enclosures. The 592 frozen ground IDs remain in immutable old
releases and are listed as deferred. The source residential foundation census
and commercial ground slots are separately accounted for.

Execute pinned source constructors, setters, insulation adjustments and effective
parallel-path calculations. Preserve source nulls, native defaults, original
units, actual getter values and reference hashes in property evidence. A target
is evidence rather than guaranteed attained performance. Model-specific material
requirements permit massless/air-gap resistance and explicit engine VT
autocalculation; they do not invent heat capacity or optional glazing summaries.

Resolve commercial source climate groups and first-match ordering exactly as
the pinned prototype caller does. Preserve source predicates without inventing
new WWR rules. Keep supported climates explicit: 56 older DOE packages do not
support 0A/0B thermal selection. Keep distinct source archetype leakage references
selectable; never average them. Preserve source-specific residential roof-pitch,
internal-mass area and incident-solar multiplier conditions as executable
assignment requirements.

## Validation and consequences

Validate Medium Office, then residential, before all eligible exports. Readiness
requires source census equality, complete selector keys/order/dependencies,
model-specific physical inputs, every executable evidence reference, source-layer
provenance dependencies, supported climate/reference resolution, and positive
SI fixed ACH. Mutation regressions reject seven formerly accepted malformed
pilot cases. All 4,751 declared commercial package/climate/reference contexts
resolve. Independent review found no remaining actionable issues after repairs.

Freeze exact UTF-8/LF bytes, schema, examples, licenses and SHA-256 manifest in
`data/construction-releases/v1.0.0`. Existing program/definition releases and raw
sources remain unchanged. Focused checks exclude site/program regeneration and
CI. The catalogue is simulation-input-ready within its recorded source
applicability; this is not an annual simulation-result parity claim.

See [the contract](../construction-json-v1-contract.md) for source setter
limitations, consumer bindings, full counts and reproduction. Site integration
and publication require a later protected-main PR and publication checks. Ground
and systems remain unfinished and no agreement is inferred from blank H/X entries.
